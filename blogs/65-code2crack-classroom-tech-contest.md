# Code2Crack: Classroom Tech's Coding Contest for Indian Students

India's IT hiring is competitive. The students who stand out at TCS, Wipro, Infosys, and Accenture aren't just the ones who studied hard — they're the ones who practiced under pressure. **Code2Crack** is Classroom Tech's answer to that challenge.

---

## What Is Code2Crack?

Code2Crack is a competitive coding contest organized by [Classroom Tech](https://classroomtech.in), designed specifically for BCA, B.Tech, MCA, and BSc CS students preparing for campus placements.

Unlike generic competitive programming contests (Codeforces, CodeChef) that can be discouraging for beginners, Code2Crack is designed to match the difficulty and format of actual placement assessment tests — TCS NQT, Infosys InfyTQ, Wipro NLTH, and similar.

---

## Who Is Code2Crack For?

- **BCA, B.Tech, MCA students** (1st year through final year)
- **Working professionals** preparing for a switch to product companies
- **Anyone preparing** for TCS Codevita, ICPC, or similar contests

---

## What Code2Crack Covers

Each Code2Crack round includes:
- **Aptitude problems** (quantitative + logical reasoning, like actual placement tests)
- **Coding problems** (2–4 problems ranging from Easy to Medium-Hard)
- **Programming logic** questions (pseudocode, time complexity, algorithm analysis)

The format mirrors TCS NQT closely — so students who practice Code2Crack are automatically preparing for the actual exam.

---

## Why Competitive Coding Matters for Placements

1. TCS Codevita (global competition, top ranks → direct placement offers)
2. Infosys HackWithInfy (winners get better package tracks)
3. ICPC (prestigious, valued by product companies globally)
4. Any coding contest rank on your resume signals aptitude to recruiters

**Satyaki Das** himself ranked **848 in TCS Codevita** — a global competition with hundreds of thousands of participants. He built Code2Crack to give students the practice environment that develops this level of skill.

---

## How Code2Crack Helps Your Career

Students who regularly participate in Code2Crack:
- Become comfortable with timed problem-solving (reduces anxiety in actual placement tests)
- Develop pattern recognition for common problem types
- Build a competitive coding profile they can cite in interviews
- Get ranking-based motivation to improve systematically
