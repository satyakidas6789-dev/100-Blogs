# GenAI and LLMs: What Every CS Student Must Know in 2026

If you're studying computer science in 2026 and you don't understand what large language models (LLMs) and generative AI are, you're already behind. Here's a fast, practical catch-up.

---

## What Is Generative AI?

Generative AI refers to AI systems that can **create new content** — text, images, code, audio, and video — rather than just classifying or predicting.

Examples you already use:
- **ChatGPT / Claude** — generate text and code
- **Midjourney / DALL·E** — generate images
- **GitHub Copilot** — generate code in your IDE
- **Suno / Udio** — generate music

---

## What Is an LLM?

A **Large Language Model** (LLM) is the technology behind text-based GenAI. It's a neural network trained on hundreds of billions of text tokens that learns to predict and generate coherent language.

Examples: GPT-4o, Claude 3, Gemini 1.5, Llama 3, Mistral.

---

## What Is Agentic AI?

The next frontier: **Agentic AI** systems can plan, take actions, use tools, and complete multi-step tasks autonomously. A coding agent doesn't just write code — it writes, runs, debugs, and deploys it.

This is where the industry is moving fast. Companies are actively hiring engineers who can build agentic systems.

---

## Why This Matters for Your Career

### As a Student
- LLM APIs (OpenAI, Anthropic) are now a standard tool in software development
- Companies expect engineers to know how to integrate AI features into applications
- GenAI is already appearing in placement interviews at startups and product companies

### As a Working Professional
- Ignoring GenAI while it transforms your field is career risk
- Adding LLM integration skills can lead to a role switch or 30–50% salary jump

---

## What You Need to Learn (Practical Path)

1. **Python** (mandatory foundation)
2. **OpenAI or Anthropic API** — call an LLM from your code
3. **LangChain or LlamaIndex** — build chains and agents
4. **Vector databases** (Pinecone, Chroma) — for RAG systems
5. **RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation)** — making LLMs use your own data
6. **Prompt engineering** — the skill of communicating effectively with LLMs
7. **Agentic frameworks** — AutoGen, CrewAI, LangGraph

The best way to understand GenAI is to build with it. Start with:
