# AI and Tech Education for Students in Jaipur and Rajasthan 2026

Jaipur is fast becoming a significant tech hub — with major IT parks, startups, and growing recruitment from Gurgaon's corridor. Here's your AI education guide for Rajasthan.

## Jaipur's Growing Tech Ecosystem

- **EPIP Zone**: Jaipur's main IT park hosting Infosys, TCS, Wipro
- **Sitapura Industrial Area**: growing tech company presence
- Startups: RailYatri, Swiggy Jaipur operations, local EdTech startups
- Government push: Rajasthan IT Day initiatives

## What Rajasthan Employers Want

Companies recruiting in Jaipur look for:
- Python, Java, SQL fundamentals
- Web development (React, Node.js) for startups
- Data analytics skills for service companies
- Communication skills for client-facing roles

## MNIT Jaipur: The Gold Standard

MNIT (Malaviya National Institute of Technology) Jaipur is the region's top engineering college:
- Requires JEE Mains rank
- Strong placement record
- AI and data science electives in B.Tech
- Active coding clubs and hackathons

If you're at MNIT, supplement with AI research projects and competitions. If you're elsewhere, supplement with focused skill building.

## Career Path for Jaipur Students

**Option A: Stay in Jaipur**
- Join companies at EPIP Zone (Infosys, Wipro)
- Work at Jaipur-based startups (growing ecosystem)
- Government IT projects (Rajasthan government digital initiatives)

**Option B: Migrate to Gurgaon/Bangalore**
- Remote work is increasingly accepted
- Jaipur talent moving to Gurgaon is a strong trend
- 2–3 years in Jaipur + transition to NCR is a common path
