# Top Aptitude Questions Asked in IT Company Placements 2026

Every major IT company's first round is an aptitude test. Here's a curated set of the most frequently asked questions with solutions.

## Section 1: Quantitative Aptitude

### Q1: Time and Work
*If A can do a job in 10 days and B in 15 days, how long together?*

**Solution:**
- A's rate: 1/10 per day
- B's rate: 1/15 per day
- Combined: 1/10 + 1/15 = 3/30 + 2/30 = 5/30 = 1/6
- Together: **6 days** ✓

### Q2: Percentage
*A price increases by 20% and then decreases by 20%. Net change?*

**Solution:**
Start with 100. After 20% increase: 120. After 20% decrease: 120 × 0.8 = 96.
**Net change: -4%** ✓

### Q3: Speed, Distance, Time
*A train 200m long crosses a pole in 10s. What is its speed in km/h?*

**Solution:**
Speed = 200m/10s = 20 m/s = 20 × 18/5 = **72 km/h** ✓

### Q4: Profit and Loss
*Cost price ₹800, selling price ₹960. Profit %?*

**Solution:**
Profit = 960 - 800 = 160. Profit % = (160/800) × 100 = **20%** ✓

## Section 2: Logical Reasoning

### Q5: Number Series
*2, 6, 12, 20, 30, ?*

**Solution:**
Differences: 4, 6, 8, 10, 12 (increasing by 2)
Next: 30 + 12 = **42** ✓

### Q6: Blood Relations
*A is B's mother. C is A's brother. D is C's father. How is D related to B?*

**Solution:**
D is father of C. C is brother of A. A is mother of B.
D is maternal grandfather's father... Actually: D → C's father, C is A's brother → D is A's father/grandfather? 
D is father of C (A's brother) → D is A's father → D is B's **maternal grandfather** ✓

### Q7: Coding-Decoding
*If CLOUD is coded as DNPVE, how is RAIN coded?*

**Solution:**
C→D (+1), L→N (+2), O→P (+1), U→V (+1), D→E (+1)
Pattern: each letter +1, except L→N (+2)?
Actually: C+1=D, L+2=N, O+1=P, U+1=V, D+1=E (L shifts by 2 because it's the 2nd letter)
R+1=S, A+2=C, I+1=J, N+1=O → **SCJO** ✓

## Section 3: Verbal Ability

### Q8: Reading Comprehension Strategy
- Read the question FIRST, then the passage
- Look for keywords from the question in the passage
- Eliminate clearly wrong options first

### Q9: Grammar — Common Errors
*Identify the error: "Neither of the students are ready."*
**Error:** "are" should be "is" — with neither/either, use singular verb.
Correct: **"Neither of the students is ready"** ✓

## Section 4: Technical MCQs

### Q10: DBMS
*What is the output of: SELECT COUNT(*) FROM emp WHERE salary > 50000?*
Counts all employees with salary > 50000 — depends on data, but tests understanding of WHERE clause with COUNT.

### Q11: OS
*What is a deadlock?*
A deadlock occurs when two or more processes wait indefinitely for resources held by each other.
