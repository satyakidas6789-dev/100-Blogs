# How to Improve Your College CGPA and Still Get Placed in 2026

CGPA is the first filter at many companies. But it's not the last — and it's not the most important. Here's what you can realistically do.

---

## The CGPA Cutoff Reality

| Company Type | Typical CGPA Cutoff |
|---|---|
| TCS (all tracks) | 6.0 |
| Infosys | 6.5 |
| Wipro | 6.0 |
| Capgemini | 6.0 |
| Accenture | 6.5 |
| Product companies (startups) | Often no cutoff — skills-based |
| FAANG / top product | Resume shortlist based, no fixed cutoff |

If you're above 6.5, you're eligible for most service company placements. Below 6.0, some doors close — but not all.

---

## How to Raise Your CGPA (If You Still Have Time)

### 1. Target the high-weightage subjects
Each semester's CGPA contribution depends on the number of credits. Identify 2–3 high-credit subjects each semester and focus disproportionately on those.

### 2. Attend internal assessments seriously
At many Indian colleges, internal marks (assignments, class tests, practicals) contribute 30–40% of total marks. These are the easiest to score in — don't waste them.

### 3. Use past papers strategically
Most college exams repeat patterns. Solve the last 5 years' papers thoroughly. Identify repeated topics and prioritize them.

### 4. Build relationships with faculty
Faculty who know your name give you the benefit of doubt in viva exams and internal assessments. Attend class, ask questions, participate.

### 5. Study smarter, not longer
Use active recall (test yourself instead of rereading) and spaced repetition. These techniques double retention with the same study time.

---

## If Your CGPA Is Already Low (Below 6.0)

Your path to employment must rely on demonstrating skills rather than credentials:

1. **Build a strong GitHub** — 3+ real projects with live demos
2. **Get an internship** (any — even unpaid) to show experience
3. **Earn recognized certifications** (AWS, Java, Python certifications add credibility)
4. **Target startups first** — they care about what you can build, not your CGPA
5. **Competitive coding** — a LeetCode rating or CodeChef rank is hard evidence of ability
6. **Open source contributions** — visible on GitHub, global credibility
