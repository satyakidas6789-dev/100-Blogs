# Most Affordable B.Tech AI Programs in India 2026

Quality AI education doesn't have to cost ₹15 LPA. Here's where you can study AI/CS affordably and still get placed.

## Government vs Private: Fee Reality

| Type | Avg Annual Fee | Placement Quality |
|------|---------------|-----------------|
| IITs/NITs | ₹80K–3 LPA | Excellent |
| State Engineering Colleges | ₹30K–80K | Varies |
| Deemed Universities | ₹1–4 LPA | Varies |
| Private Colleges | ₹1–6 LPA | Varies |

## Most Affordable Government Options

### 1. State Engineering Colleges (West Bengal)
- Jadavpur University — ₹10K–50K per year (government-funded)
- MAKAUT-affiliated colleges — ₹50K–1 LPA
- Calcutta Institute of Technology — ₹60K/year

### 2. NIT System
- NITs across India: ₹1.25 LPA for B.Tech
- Requires JEE Advanced/Mains rank
- Scholarships available based on income

### 3. Central Universities
- Delhi Technological University — ₹1.5 LPA
- NIT Durgapur — ₹1.25 LPA
- Thapar University — ₹2 LPA (deemed, but scholarship-heavy)

## Scholarship Options That Cut Costs Further

- **Central Sector Scholarship**: ₹12,000/year for top students
- **AICTE National Scholarship**: merit-based, ₹25,000/year
- **State government scholarships**: check your state portal
- **College merit scholarships**: often 25–50% fee waiver
