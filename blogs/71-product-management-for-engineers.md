# Product Management for Engineers — How to Transition from Dev to PM in 2026

Software engineers often ask: "Should I go deeper into tech, or move into product management?" Here's the complete honest guide.

## What Does a Product Manager Actually Do?

A PM is the "CEO of a product." They:
- Define what to build (and what NOT to build)
- Work with engineers, designers, data analysts
- Prioritize features using data and user research
- Own the product roadmap
- Drive business metrics (revenue, retention, engagement)

## Why Engineers Make Great PMs

Your coding background gives you an unfair advantage:
- You understand technical constraints
- You speak the same language as the dev team
- You can evaluate feasibility in real-time
- You can read data and logs independently

## Skills You Need to Develop

### From Engineering Background (Already Have)
✅ Technical understanding  
✅ Logical problem-solving  
✅ Reading data/analytics  

### Need to Develop
- **User empathy**: think from the customer's perspective
- **Business acumen**: understand revenue, CAC, LTV, churn
- **Communication**: write PRDs, present to stakeholders
- **Prioritization frameworks**: RICE, MoSCoW, Kano Model
- **A/B testing**: design and interpret experiments

## PM Transition Roadmap

**Phase 1 (Month 1-2): Learn PM Fundamentals**
- Read: "Inspired" by Marty Cagan, "The Lean Startup"
- Take: PM courses on Coursera or LinkedIn Learning
- Shadow: find a PM at your company and shadow them

**Phase 2 (Month 3-4): Build PM Portfolio**
- Write product teardowns (analyze Swiggy, Zepto, Zomato)
- Create mock PRDs for apps you use daily
- Analyze product metrics using public data

**Phase 3 (Month 5-6): Get the Role**
- Apply for Associate PM (APM) programs
- Target startups (easier PM entry)
- Prepare for PM interviews: case studies, metrics, estimation

## Top Companies with APM Programs in India

- **Google APM Program** (competitive, IIT/IIM bias)
- **Microsoft PM Internship** (strong for engineering backgrounds)
- **Swiggy, Zomato, Meesho** (good for product thinking)
- **TCS, Infosys product divisions** (internal mobility)

## Salary Expectations

| Level | Experience | Salary |
|-------|-----------|--------|
| APM / Junior PM | 0–2 years | ₹12–20 LPA |
| PM | 2–5 years | ₹20–40 LPA |
| Senior PM | 5–8 years | ₹35–60 LPA |
| Director of Product | 8+ years | ₹60 LPA+ |

## AI Skills for PMs in 2026

Modern PMs must understand AI products:
- How LLMs work (conceptually)
- How to evaluate AI feature quality
- Ethics and hallucination risks in AI products
- AI-first product thinking

## Is PM Right for You?

Go PM if you:
- Love users more than code
- Enjoy ambiguity and strategy
- Want business-level impact

Stay engineering if you:
- Love deep technical problems
- Prefer clear solutions over fuzzy strategy
- Want to build AI/ML systems directly
