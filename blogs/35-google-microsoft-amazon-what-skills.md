# Google, Microsoft, Amazon Hiring: What Skills Do They Actually Look For?

FAANG (or MAANG) jobs are the aspiration of millions of CS students. The hiring bars are high but well-defined. Here's exactly what these companies look for.

---

## The Common Thread Across All Three

Despite different company cultures, Google, Microsoft, and Amazon test three things:

1. **Algorithmic problem-solving** (DSA) — consistent across all three
2. **System design** — for SDE-2 and above; increasingly asked even for freshers
3. **Behavioral interviews** — structured differently per company

---

## Google's Hiring Process

- **Rounds:** 1 phone screen + 4–5 on-site (virtual) rounds
- **DSA focus:** Medium to Hard LeetCode. Graph problems, tree traversals, DP, string manipulation
- **System design:** Large-scale distributed systems — design Gmail, Google Maps, YouTube
- **Behavioral:** "Googleyness" — curiosity, collaboration, intellectual humility
- **What stands out:** Exceptional problem-solving speed AND ability to communicate thought process clearly

---

## Microsoft's Hiring Process

- **Rounds:** 4–5 technical rounds + 1 HR
- **DSA focus:** Medium LeetCode, less emphasis on Hard than Google
- **System design:** Moderate complexity — design a parking lot, URL shortener, notification system
- **Behavioral:** Respect, accountability, growth mindset (Microsoft's core culture)
- **What stands out:** Clean code, good communication, demonstrated product thinking

---

## Amazon's Hiring Process

- **Rounds:** Online assessment + 5–6 interview rounds (loop)
- **DSA focus:** Medium LeetCode — arrays, trees, graphs, greedy
- **System design:** Required for SDE-2+ — design DoorDash, Amazon warehouse system
- **Leadership Principles (LPs):** This is Amazon's uniqueness. You MUST prepare 2–3 stories per LP
  - Customer Obsession, Bias for Action, Dive Deep, Deliver Results, Invent and Simplify (14 total)
- **What stands out:** Data-driven thinking and concrete examples using the STAR format

---

## How to Prepare: 6–12 Month Plan

| Milestone | Activity |
|---|---|
| Month 1–3 | Solve 150 Easy/Medium LeetCode systematically by topic |
| Month 4–5 | Solve 75 Medium/Hard. Practice under time pressure |
| Month 5–6 | System design: read Grokking the System Design Interview, design 10 systems |
| Month 6–8 | Mock interviews (at least 20 rounds with a partner or platform) |
| Month 8–9 | Behavioral prep (for Amazon: write out all 14 LP stories) |
| Month 9–12 | Apply, interview, iterate |

---

## Realistic Expectations

- Most successful candidates apply 3–5 times over 2–3 years before landing a FAANG role
- IIT/NIT brand helps for initial shortlisting but does NOT guarantee success
- Many FAANG engineers in India come from average colleges — skills + persistence win
