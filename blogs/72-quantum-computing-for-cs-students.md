# Quantum Computing for CS Students — What You Need to Know in 2026

"Should I learn quantum computing?" — students ask this after seeing job postings at IBM, Google, and TCS Quantum. Here's the honest answer.

## What Is Quantum Computing?

Classical computers use **bits** (0 or 1). Quantum computers use **qubits**, which can be 0, 1, or both simultaneously (superposition). This allows them to solve certain problems exponentially faster.

Key concepts:
- **Superposition**: qubits exist in multiple states simultaneously
- **Entanglement**: qubits can be correlated across distances
- **Interference**: amplify correct answers, cancel wrong ones

## What Quantum Computers Can Actually Do (2026)

Current reality:
- Still in **NISQ era** (Noisy Intermediate-Scale Quantum)
- Useful for: optimization problems, drug discovery simulations, cryptography
- NOT useful for: general computing, replacing classical software

Major players: IBM Quantum, Google Quantum AI, IonQ, D-Wave, Microsoft Azure Quantum

## Career Opportunities in India

Currently limited but growing:
- **TCS Quantum Computing Lab** — hired 200+ quantum researchers
- **Infosys Quantum Hub** — partnerships with IBM Quantum
- **IIT research programs** — postdoc, PhD positions
- **Startups**: BosonQ Psi (Pune), QpiAI (Bangalore)

Average salaries: ₹15–40 LPA for quantum engineers in India (rare roles, high pay)

## Should YOU Learn Quantum Computing Now?

**Yes, if you are:**
- Doing MTech/PhD in CS, Physics, or Math
- Interested in research careers
- Already strong in linear algebra and classical computing

**Focus on classical AI/ML first, if you are:**
- BCA or B.Tech (1st–3rd year)
- Looking for immediate placement
- Building your first technical career

## Learning Path for Curious Students

**Prerequisites** (must have first):
- Linear algebra (matrices, eigenvalues)
- Python programming
- Basic probability and statistics
- Classical algorithms and data structures

**Quantum learning path:**
1. IBM Qiskit Textbook (free online)
2. Microsoft Quantum Katas
3. Coursera: "Introduction to Quantum Computing" (St. Petersburg)
4. Build basic circuits on IBM Quantum Experience (free cloud access)

## Quantum + AI = Quantum ML

The intersection of quantum and AI is **Quantum Machine Learning (QML)**:
- Quantum neural networks
- Faster optimization for ML training
- Quantum advantage for specific ML tasks

This is a research area, not production-ready yet.
