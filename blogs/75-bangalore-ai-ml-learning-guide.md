# AI ML Learning Guide for Students and Professionals in Bangalore 2026

Bangalore is India's startup capital and the home of AI research in the country. If you're here, you're in the right place. Here's how to make the most of it.

## Bangalore's AI Ecosystem

- **IISc Bangalore**: top AI/ML research institution in India
- **IIIT Bangalore**: dedicated AI and software engineering programs
- **Startups**: Flipkart AI, Swiggy ML, Meesho DS, 100+ AI-first startups
- **MNCs**: Google, IBM Research, SAP Labs, Oracle, Microsoft

The ecosystem means opportunities — but also high competition. Skills matter more than ever.

## For Working Professionals in Bangalore

Bangalore professionals have unique needs:
- Many already work at MNCs and want to move to AI roles internally
- Startup culture demands hands-on AI skills, not just theory
- Weekend or evening programs are essential

**What to focus on:**
- MLOps (deploying models in production)
- LLM fine-tuning and RAG architectures
- Python + PyTorch/TensorFlow
- Cloud ML (AWS SageMaker, GCP Vertex AI)

## Action Plan for Bangalore Students

1. **Month 1**: Python + SQL fundamentals (if not already solid)
2. **Month 2-3**: ML with scikit-learn, build 2 projects
3. **Month 4**: Deep learning (PyTorch or TensorFlow)
4. **Month 5**: Deploy a model on AWS/GCP
5. **Month 6**: Apply to Bangalore startups and MNCs
