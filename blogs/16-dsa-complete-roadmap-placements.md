# DSA Complete Roadmap for Placements: Everything You Need in 2026

DSA is the most tested topic in software engineering interviews — for freshers at TCS, for mid-level roles at product companies, and for senior positions at FAANG. Here's your complete roadmap.

---

## Why DSA Is Non-Negotiable

Every company with a competitive hiring process tests DSA because:
1. It measures problem-solving ability, not just syntax knowledge
2. It's a fair filter — knowledge of DSA is learnable by anyone
3. Efficient code matters at scale — companies need engineers who understand time and space complexity

---

## The DSA Roadmap (By Topic)

### Level 1: Fundamentals (Weeks 1–4)
- Arrays and Strings
- Time and Space Complexity (Big O)
- Two Pointers
- Sliding Window
- Prefix Sums

### Level 2: Core Data Structures (Weeks 5–10)
- Linked Lists (singly, doubly, circular)
- Stacks and Queues
- Hashing and HashMaps
- Trees (binary tree, BST, AVL, segment tree)
- Heaps and Priority Queues

### Level 3: Algorithms (Weeks 11–16)
- Sorting algorithms (merge sort, quick sort)
- Binary Search (and its variations)
- Recursion and Backtracking
- Graph algorithms (BFS, DFS, Dijkstra, Bellman-Ford)
- Dynamic Programming (DP) — the most important and hardest

### Level 4: Advanced Topics (Weeks 17–20)
- Tries
- Segment Trees and Fenwick Trees
- Topological Sort
- Advanced DP patterns

## Practice Schedule

| Day | Activity |
|---|---|
| Weekday 1-2 | Learn a new topic (theory + 3 easy problems) |
| Weekday 3-5 | Solve 2 medium problems on the topic |
| Weekend | Mock interview (2 hours) + review weak areas |

**Target:** 200–300 problems over 5–6 months

---

## Company-Specific Focus

| Company | DSA Level Required |
|---|---|
| TCS, Infosys, Wipro | Easy–Medium (patterns, arrays, basic DP) |
| Capgemini, Accenture | Easy–Medium + aptitude |
| Startups (mid-stage) | Medium–Hard |
| Amazon, Microsoft | Medium–Hard + System Design |
| Google, Meta | Hard + Advanced |
