# Interview Preparation in Kolkata: What Every Student Should Know

Getting placed in Kolkata's IT market requires the same skills as anywhere in India — but Kolkata students have specific advantages and specific gaps. Here's what you need to know.

---

## Kolkata's IT Job Market in 2026

**Major IT employers in Kolkata:**
- TCS (Salt Lake Sector V — massive presence)
- Wipro (Kolkata campus)
- Cognizant (DLF area)
- Tech Mahindra
- ITC Infotech
- Mphasis
- Various startups in the New Town ecosystem

**Most recruited roles:** Java developer, Python developer, full stack, QA engineer, data analyst, business analyst

---

## What Kolkata Recruiters Specifically Test

**TCS Kolkata recruiting pattern:**
- NQT (National Qualifier Test): Aptitude + Programming Logic + Coding
- Technical interview: OOPs, Java basics, data structures
- HR interview: Communication skills (very important — recruiters note local language comfort with English)

**Key gap for Kolkata students:** Communication skills in English during interviews. Technical skills are often strong; fluency and confidence in technical English requires practice.

---

## The Three Pillars of Interview Preparation

### 1. Technical Preparation
- DSA: 200+ LeetCode problems (Easy to Medium)
- Language: Java or Python — know one deeply
- Projects: 2 real projects with live deployments

### 2. Aptitude Preparation
- Quantitative aptitude (Kolkata students often underinvest here)
- Logical reasoning and verbal ability
- Practice: IndiaBix, PrepInsta, RS Aggarwal

### 3. Communication and Mock Interviews
This is where most Kolkata students need the most work:
- Practice technical explanations in English daily
- Record yourself explaining a project or a concept
- Do mock interviews with real feedback
