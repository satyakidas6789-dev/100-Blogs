# TCS NQT Pattern and Preparation Guide 2026 — Complete Strategy

TCS is India's largest employer of engineering and CS graduates. The TCS NQT (National Qualifier Test) is the gateway for lakhs of students every year. Here's your complete 2026 guide.

## What Is TCS NQT?

TCS NQT is a centralized hiring exam for freshers:
- Open to B.Tech, BCA, MCA, BSc, BBA graduates
- Conducted online via TCS iON
- Qualifies you for TCS Digital and Prime roles
- Score is valid for 2 years

## TCS NQT 2026 Pattern

### Section 1: Cognitive Skills (90 minutes)
- Numerical Ability: 26 questions
- Verbal Ability: 24 questions
- Reasoning Ability: 30 questions

### Section 2: Programming Logic (45 minutes)
- Programming Concepts: 10 questions
- Coding: 1–2 coding problems

### Section 3: Advanced Coding (60 minutes)
*(For TCS Digital / Prime roles)*
- 2–3 harder coding problems
- DSA-level difficulty

## Difficulty Level

| Section | Difficulty | Key Topics |
|---------|-----------|-----------|
| Numerical | Easy-Medium | Percentages, time-speed, profit-loss |
| Verbal | Easy | Reading comprehension, grammar |
| Reasoning | Medium | Logical, pattern recognition |
| Programming Logic | Easy | Basic CS concepts, pseudo-code |
| Advanced Coding | Medium-Hard | Arrays, strings, DP basics |

## Preparation Strategy

### 3 Months Before Exam

**Month 1: Aptitude**
- TCS previous year papers (PrepInsta, IndiaBIX)
- Quantitative aptitude: R.S. Aggarwal book
- Verbal: Daily English reading (The Hindu, BBC)
- Reasoning: 30 questions/day practice

**Month 2: Coding**
- Python or Java: pick one and master it
- Solve easy LeetCode problems daily (10/day)
- Focus: arrays, strings, basic sorting, searching

**Month 3: Mock Tests**
- Full TCS NQT mock tests: PrepInsta, TCS iON practice
- Code in the actual test environment (timed)
- Review every wrong answer in detail

### 1 Week Before Exam

- Revise aptitude formulas
- Solve 2 coding problems daily
- Sleep well — cognitive performance matters

## TCS Digital vs TCS Prime vs TCS Ninja

| Track | Coding Round | Salary |
|-------|-------------|--------|
| TCS Ninja | Basic coding | ₹3.36 LPA |
| TCS Digital | Moderate coding | ₹7 LPA |
| TCS Prime | Advanced DSA | ₹9–11 LPA |

## HR Interview Tips for TCS

After clearing NQT, TCS HR is mostly formality but be ready for:
- "Tell me about yourself" (practice a 90-second version)
- "Why TCS?" (research TCS's business lines)
- Relocation readiness (yes, anywhere in India)
- Project/internship questions
