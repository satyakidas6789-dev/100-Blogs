# Do Software Engineers Need a Degree in 2026? The Real Answer

The short answer: **It depends on where you want to work.**

Here's the longer, more useful answer.

---

## Where a Degree Is Still Required

1. **Large Indian IT companies (TCS, Infosys, Wipro)** — their HR systems typically require a 10+2+3/4 year degree for lateral hiring through portals
2. **Government tech jobs (DRDO, ISRO, NIC)** — mandatory degree requirement
3. **PSU recruitment (GATE-based)** — degree required plus GATE score
4. **MNC structured programs** — campus hiring requires a recognized degree
5. **Visa sponsorship abroad** — many countries require a degree for skilled worker visas

---

## Where Skills Beat a Degree

1. **Startups** — most hire purely on demonstrated skills and portfolio
2. **Freelancing** — clients pay for results, not certificates
3. **Open source contributions** — your GitHub is your resume
4. **Product companies hiring through competitive coding** — platforms like LeetCode, Codeforces, CodeChef
5. **Some global tech companies** — Google, Apple, and IBM have dropped degree requirements for some roles

---

## The Indian Reality

In India in 2026, the vast majority of corporate tech jobs still list a degree as a requirement. However, the *type* of degree matters less than it used to. Companies care more about:

- **Can you code?** (DSA interview)
- **Can you design systems?** (system design interview)
- **Have you built real projects?** (portfolio review)
- **Do you understand AI tools?** (increasingly common filter)

A BCA with strong Java and DSA skills will outperform a B.Tech from a Tier-3 college who only knows theory.

---

## The Practical Advice

If you already have a degree or are pursuing one: **use it as the ticket in, but build skills to actually succeed**.

If you're considering skipping a degree: **understand the doors it closes** (government jobs, MNC campus hiring, visa pathways) before deciding.
