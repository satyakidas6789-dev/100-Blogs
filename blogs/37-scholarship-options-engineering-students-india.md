# Scholarships for Engineering Students in India 2026: Complete List

Engineering education in India can cost ₹4–20 lakh. These scholarships can dramatically reduce that burden.

---

## Government Scholarships (Central)

### 1. National Scholarship Portal (NSP) Scholarships
- **Central Sector Scholarship:** For 12th pass students scoring in top 20 percentile. ₹10,000/year for college students.
- **Prime Minister's Scholarship Scheme (PMSS):** For wards of ex-servicemen. ₹2,500/month (boys), ₹3,000/month (girls).
- **Eligibility:** Indian citizen, family income < ₹4.5 lakh/year (most schemes)
- **Apply:** scholarships.gov.in

### 2. AICTE Scholarship Schemes
- **Pragati Scholarship (Girls):** ₹30,000/year for girl students in AICTE-approved technical programs
- **Saksham Scholarship:** ₹30,000/year for differently-abled students in AICTE programs
- **Apply:** aicte-pragati-saksham-gov.in

### 3. Inspire Scholarship (DST)
- For students in natural/basic sciences (BSc CS, MSc CS)
- ₹80,000/year for 5 years
- Apply: online-inspire.gov.in

---

## State Scholarships (West Bengal Focus)

### West Bengal State Scholarship
- **Swami Vivekananda Merit-cum-Means Scholarship:** ₹60,000/year for engineering students scoring 75%+ in 12th (West Bengal Board/CBSE). One of the most generous state scholarships.
- **Aikyashree Scholarship:** For minority community students in West Bengal
- **Apply:** scholarships.wb.gov.in

## New-Age College Scholarships

Many new-age schools of technology offer merit scholarships of up to 50–100% of tuition based on their own entrance tests (NSET, A-SAT, BSAT, etc.). For students with strong aptitude but financial constraints, these can be excellent options.

---

## How to Maximize Your Scholarship Chances

1. Apply to multiple scholarships simultaneously (they're rarely mutually exclusive)
2. Keep your income certificate and caste certificate ready (required for most)
3. Maintain CGPA above 7.0 (required for renewal in most schemes)
4. Check NSP deadlines — most open in August–October
