# AI and Tech Careers for Students in Delhi NCR 2026

The Delhi NCR corridor (Noida, Gurgaon, Faridabad, Greater Noida) is one of India's largest tech employment hubs. Here's your complete AI career guide for the region.

## Delhi NCR's Tech Ecosystem

- **Noida/Greater Noida**: HCL, Wipro, Samsung Research, major IT parks
- **Gurgaon**: MNCs (Microsoft, Google India HQ, Deloitte, Accenture)
- **Delhi**: Government tech projects, DRDO, media tech
- Startup hubs: Gurugram (Paytm, Zomato founding city), Noida tech parks

## AI/ML Opportunities in NCR

**Gurgaon** (highest-paying):
- Consulting firms (Deloitte AI, EY Tech, KPMG): AI Analyst roles, ₹8–15 LPA
- Microsoft India: SDE, ML Engineer, ₹20–40 LPA
- Startups: UrbanCompany, Dream11 (tech office)

**Noida**:
- HCL Technologies: AI Engineer roles
- Samsung Research India: vision AI, NLP
- Paytm: recommendation systems, fraud detection

## What Delhi NCR Recruiters Want

Gurgaon consulting firms prioritize:
- Problem-solving and communication
- Excel + Python + SQL
- Understanding of business use cases for AI

Tech MNCs (Microsoft, Google Gurgaon) want:
- DSA mastery (LeetCode hard level)
- System design
- ML fundamentals + cloud deployment

## Study Plan for NCR Students

**In B.Tech/BCA (Years 1-3)**:
- Join college coding club (if none, start one)
- Complete DSA roadmap (150+ LeetCode problems)
- Get AI/ML certification by Year 3

**Working professional in NCR**:
- Evening/weekend online upskilling
- Target internal AI projects at your employer
- Transition to AI roles within 6–12 months
