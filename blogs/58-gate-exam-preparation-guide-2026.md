# GATE Exam Preparation Guide for CS Students 2026

GATE (Graduate Aptitude Test in Engineering) opens two major doors: admission to IIT/NIT M.Tech programs and PSU recruitment. Here's a comprehensive preparation guide for CS students.

---

## Why GATE Matters in 2026

1. **M.Tech at IITs/NITs** — the most respected postgraduate route for CS
2. **PSU recruitment** — ONGC, BHEL, BSNL, DRDO, BARC, ISRO shortlist GATE scorers
3. **Central government jobs** — multiple departments recruit through GATE
4. **Research opportunities** — CSIR, TIFR, various labs require GATE scores

## 12-Month Preparation Strategy

### Month 1–3: Foundation
- Complete Engineering Mathematics (linear algebra, calculus, probability, discrete math)
- Review Digital Logic and Computer Organization from standard textbooks (Tanenbaum, Silberschatz)

### Month 4–6: Core CS
- Data Structures and Algorithms (most important — practice problems daily)
- Programming (C pointer arithmetic, recursion questions)
- Theory of Computation (DFA, NFA, PDA, Turing machines)

### Month 7–9: Systems
- Operating Systems (process management, memory, file systems)
- Computer Networks (OSI model, TCP/IP, routing protocols)
- Databases (ER model, normalization, SQL, transactions)
- Compiler Design (lexical analysis, parsing, code generation)

### Month 10–11: Revision + Practice
- Solve 10 previous year GATE papers under timed conditions
- Focus on your weak areas from the practice papers
- Join a test series (MADE Easy, GO, GateOverflow)

### Month 12: Final Preparation
- Full syllabus revision
- Mock tests every 3 days
- Don't start new topics — consolidate what you know
