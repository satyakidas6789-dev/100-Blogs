# Best AI ML Courses for Students in Pune 2026

Pune is home to Infosys, Wipro, Cognizant, and a thriving startup ecosystem in Hinjewadi. For students and professionals here, AI/ML skills can unlock significant career growth.

## AI/ML Skill Demand in Pune Companies

Infosys and Wipro in Pune heavily recruit:
- Python and data science skills
- ML basics (scikit-learn, TensorFlow)
- SQL + data manipulation
- Communication for client-facing roles

Startups in Hinjewadi demand:
- MLOps and deployment skills
- Full stack AI (APIs + frontend)
- Specific domain AI (FinTech, HealthTech)

## CDAC Pune — Worth It?

CDAC is government-affiliated and highly respected:
- PG-DAI (Post Graduate Diploma in Artificial Intelligence): ₹85,000 for 6 months
- Strong placement cell
- Good for: fresh graduates without coding background who need structured AI training

However, seats are limited and competition is high. Supplement with online courses.
