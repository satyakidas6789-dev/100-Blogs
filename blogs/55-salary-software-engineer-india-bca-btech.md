## BCA vs B.Tech — Does It Affect Salary?

For most service companies: **No significant difference** if skills are equal.  
TCS, Infosys, and Wipro hire BCA and B.Tech graduates at the same track.

For product companies: **B.Tech from a known college may get easier shortlist** — but skills in the interview determine the offer.

With MCA after BCA: Salary outcomes are comparable to B.Tech in almost all roles.

---

## What Actually Moves Your Salary

1. **DSA level** — the primary filter at product companies
2. **Tech stack expertise** — Java Spring Boot, Python + ML, React + Node
3. **System design** — unlocks senior roles
4. **AI/ML skills** — 30–50% salary premium over equivalent non-AI roles
5. **Domain knowledge** — fintech, healthtech specialists earn more

---

## The ₹10 LPA Benchmark

Getting to ₹10 LPA as a fresher requires:
- Strong DSA (solve 200+ LeetCode problems)
- 2–3 real projects with deployment
- Either good college brand OR exceptional technical skill to compensate
