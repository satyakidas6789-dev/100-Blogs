Hyderabad has transformed into one of India's top tech hubs — home to Google, Microsoft, Amazon, and thousands of startups. Students here have access to both great colleges and great upskilling options. Here's your guide.

- **HITEC City**: India's Silicon Valley equivalent
- Major offices: Microsoft, Google, Amazon, Infosys, TCS, Wipro
- Startup ecosystem: 1,500+ tech startups
- Strong engineering college network: JNTU, BITS Hyderabad, IIIT Hyderabad
