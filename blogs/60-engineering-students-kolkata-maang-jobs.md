# How Engineering Students from Kolkata Are Breaking Into MAANG Companies

Kolkata has produced some of India's most accomplished software engineers. IIT Kharagpur alumni lead teams at Google and Amazon globally. Jadavpur University graduates are at Microsoft and Meta. But what about students from other Kolkata colleges?

Here's the realistic picture — and the path that's actually working.

---

## The Reality of MAANG From Non-IIT Kolkata Colleges

**Honest assessment:** The IIT Kharagpur pipeline to MAANG is strong and well-established. For students from Heritage, Techno Main, MAKAUT-affiliated colleges, and similar institutions, the path exists but requires much more self-driven preparation.

The good news: companies like Google, Amazon, and Microsoft have explicitly stated they hire based on skills and interviews, not college. The bar is the same for everyone once you're in the interview room.

---

## What Differentiates Successful Kolkata Students

The students from non-IIT Kolkata colleges who land MAANG or near-MAANG roles share these traits:

**1. Started preparation early (Year 1 or 2)**  
Competitive DSA preparation takes 12–18 months of consistent practice. Starting in final year is too late for MAANG-level roles.

**2. Strong LeetCode profile**  
Most successful candidates solve 400–600 LeetCode problems, with a significant portion being Medium and Hard.

**3. Open source contributions or strong GitHub**  
Projects that show system thinking and software engineering maturity — not just tutorials.

**4. Competitive coding credentials**  
ICPC participation, strong CodeChef/Codeforces ratings, Codevita performance — these open eyes at MAANG recruiters.

**5. Networking through LinkedIn and referrals**  
Many Kolkata students land MAANG interviews through alumni referrals. LinkedIn outreach to IIT Kharagpur or Jadavpur University alumni at target companies is a real strategy.

---

## The Preparation Blueprint

| Year | Focus |
|---|---|
| Year 1 | C/Java/Python basics + competitive coding start |
| Year 2 | DSA (LeetCode 150 problems) + first real project |
| Year 3 | System design intro + 300+ LeetCode + internship |
| Year 4 | 500+ LeetCode + 3 projects + mock interviews + apply |
