# Highest Package for CS Freshers in India 2026: What's Realistic?

LinkedIn is full of "I got ₹80 LPA!" posts. Your college placement brochure shows "highest package ₹42 LPA." But what does the average fresher actually earn? Here's an honest breakdown.

---

## The Honest Salary Tiers (Freshers, 2026)

### Tier 1: IIT / Top NIT → Product Companies
- Google, Microsoft, Amazon, Meta, Uber: ₹40–1 Cr+
- Indian Unicorns (Swiggy, Meesho, PhonePe): ₹20–45 LPA
- Mid-stage startups: ₹12–25 LPA

### Tier 2: Good Private Colleges → Strong Skills
- Indian product companies: ₹10–20 LPA
- Well-funded startups: ₹8–15 LPA
- IT services (Capgemini, L&T Infotech, Mphasis): ₹5–8 LPA

### Tier 3: Average Colleges → Service Companies
- TCS, Infosys, Wipro (track offers): ₹3.5–6 LPA
- TCS Digital / Prime tracks: ₹7–9 LPA
- TCS Ninja: ₹3.5–4 LPA

### Tier 4: BCA / MCA Freshers → Service + SME
- IT services, KPOs, SME tech companies: ₹2.5–5 LPA
- Strong skills → same as B.Tech in most openings: ₹4–8 LPA

---

## What Actually Drives Your Package

1. **College pedigree** — the first filter, especially at product companies
2. **DSA skill level** — determines which tier of companies you can clear
3. **Project quality** — what you built, not just that you built something
4. **Communication** — HR rounds eliminate technically sound but unclear communicators
5. **AI/ML skills** — increasingly differentiating even at service companies

---

## How Students Jump Tiers

Students from average colleges consistently landing ₹10–15 LPA have these in common:
- Solved 250+ LeetCode problems (Medium and Hard)
- Built 2–3 real projects with live deployments
- Contributed to open source or won a hackathon
- Prepared for 3–6 months with structured mock interviews

---

## The LPA Question: Internship Stipend

Many freshers overlook internship quality as a salary driver:
- A ₹30,000–50,000/month internship often converts to a ₹6–10 LPA PPO
- Companies like Razorpay, Groww, and CRED actively convert interns
- Service companies also give PPO offers through their internship programs
