# How to Get an Internship with Stipend as a BCA/B.Tech Student in 2026

Paid internships are the fastest track to a full-time offer — and they're available to students from any college if you approach it right. Here's how.

---

## Why Internships Matter More Than Ever

- Most top companies now hire 30–50% of freshers via internship conversions (Pre-Placement Offers)
- A paid internship at ₹25,000–50,000/month validates your skills better than any certificate
- Internship experience jumps your salary expectations by ₹1–3 LPA as a fresher

---

## Where to Find Internships (India, 2026)

| Platform | Best For |
|---|---|
| Internshala | Volume — thousands of listings, many stipend-based |
| LinkedIn | Startup and mid-size company internships |
| Naukri.com | IT company internships, including TCS, Wipro |
| AngelList / Wellfound | Startup internships (often high stipend) |
| Company career pages | Direct applications (often unconverified on job boards) |
| GitHub + open source | Showing skills → companies reach out to you |
| Referrals | Best conversion rate — ask seniors and alumni |

---

## Minimum Skills to Land a Paid Internship

For most startups and product companies:
- **One programming language** you can code in confidently (Java, Python, or JavaScript)
- **One real project** on GitHub (not a tutorial copy — something you built)
- **Basic Git** — commit, push, pull request
- **Understanding of REST APIs** (for backend roles)

For service company internships (TCS NextStep, Infosys Internship):
- Resume with 7.0+ CGPA
- Basic aptitude + coding assessment pass

---

## How to Cold Apply and Get Responses

Most students send generic applications and get ignored. Stand out:

1. **Personalize every application** — mention a specific product/feature you find interesting
2. **Include GitHub link** with at least 1 real project
3. **Keep the email short** (3 sentences) — what you offer, why this company, your GitHub link
4. **Apply to 50+ companies** — internship hiring is high-volume; expect 5–10% response rate

---

## Convert Your Internship to a Job

During your internship:
- Ask clarifying questions, don't just execute tasks
- Deliver on time, every time
- Go slightly beyond what's asked (add one extra feature, write tests)
- Build relationships with team members
- Ask mid-internship: "Am I on track to get a PPO?"

A well-executed internship has an 60–80% PPO conversion rate at most companies.
