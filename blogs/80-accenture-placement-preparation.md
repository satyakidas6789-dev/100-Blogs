# Accenture Placement Preparation 2026 — Complete Guide for Freshers

Accenture hires 50,000+ freshers annually in India. Their selection process is well-structured. Here's how to crack it in 2026.

## Accenture 2026 Hiring Tracks

1. **Associate Software Engineer (ASE)**: ₹4.5 LPA, standard track
2. **Packaged App Development Associate**: ₹4.5 LPA, ERP/SAP focus
3. **Technology Analyst (direct)**: ₹6.5 LPA, for high scorers

## Accenture Selection Process 2026

### Round 1: Online Assessment (80 minutes)
- **Cognitive & Technical Assessment**: Logical, numerical, verbal
- **Coding Assessment**: 2 problems in Python/Java/C++
- Conducted on AMCAT or Accenture's own platform

### Round 2: Communication Assessment
- Spoken English test (auto-evaluated)
- Reading passage + response
- Very important — Accenture is a client-facing company

### Round 3: HR Interview (30 minutes)
- Mostly behavioral questions
- Project discussion
- Relocation and joining willingness

## Online Test Preparation

**Cognitive Assessment:**
- Verbal: reading comprehension, sentence completion
- Logical: series, analogies, data interpretation  
- Numerical: percentages, ratios, averages

**Coding Assessment:**
- Difficulty: Easy to Medium
- Languages: Python preferred (shorter code)
- Focus: arrays, strings, basic sorting

Top resources:
- PrepInsta Accenture practice papers
- GeeksForGeeks basic problems
- HackerRank 30 Days of Code

## Communication Assessment Tips

This is where many students fail despite good tech skills:
1. Speak clearly and at a moderate pace
2. Use complete sentences (avoid one-word answers)
3. Practice reading paragraphs aloud
4. Record yourself — listen for grammar errors
5. Don't memorize scripts; practice natural fluency

## HR Interview Questions

Common questions:
- "Tell me about yourself"
- "Why Accenture?" (Research Accenture's AI/cloud business)
- "Where do you see yourself in 5 years?"
- "Tell me about a challenge you overcame"
- "Are you okay with night shifts or rotational shifts?"

**Prepare your answer to "Why Accenture?" properly:**
Mention: Accenture's AI/cloud practice, their projects in India, their training programs (like the Accenture Technology Academy).
