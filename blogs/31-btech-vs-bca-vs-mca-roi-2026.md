# B.Tech vs BCA vs MCA: Which Degree Has the Best ROI in 2026?

Three degrees. Different entry points. Different costs. Different outcomes. Here's the honest comparison.

---

## The Quick Comparison Table

| Factor | B.Tech (CSE) | BCA | BCA + MCA |
|---|---|---|---|
| Duration | 4 years | 3 years | 3 + 2 = 5 years |
| Fee range | ₹4–20 lakh total | ₹1–5 lakh total | ₹2–8 lakh total |
| JEE required | For NITs/IITs (yes) | No | No |
| Starting salary | ₹3.5–8 LPA | ₹2.5–5 LPA | ₹4–8 LPA |
| 5-year salary potential | ₹12–25 LPA | ₹8–18 LPA | ₹10–22 LPA |
| Government jobs | Yes (GATE eligible) | Limited | Yes (GATE eligible) |
| Core CS depth | High | Medium | High |

---

## ROI Analysis

### B.Tech at Private College (₹8 lakh fees, ₹3.5 LPA start)
- Cost: ₹8 lakh + 4 years
- Break-even: ~3 years after graduation
- **ROI: Medium** (if college has strong placement; Poor at weak colleges)

### BCA (₹3 lakh fees, ₹3 LPA start)
- Cost: ₹3 lakh + 3 years
- Break-even: ~1.5 years
- **ROI: High** (lower cost, faster break-even)

### BCA + MCA (₹6 lakh total, ₹5 LPA start)
- Cost: ₹6 lakh + 5 years
- Break-even: ~2 years post-MCA
- **ROI: Highest** for non-IIT students (strong technical foundation, same software jobs)

---

## The Skill Multiplier (What Really Changes ROI)

Regardless of which degree you choose, the biggest ROI multiplier is **practical skills**:
- A BCA graduate with Java + DSA + 3 real projects outearns a B.Tech with no skills
- An MCA with AI/ML skills gets ₹10+ LPA at companies that don't care about your degree name

**Satyaki Das** himself followed BCA → MCA → MTech, then built a career at TCS — consistently one of India's largest tech employers. His path proves this route works.
